The boundary

Stump Math keeps pass, chip and schedule arithmetic only. It gives no verdicts about what your grinder can safely take - what the machine can handle is the manufacturer's call. Follow the manual's capacity and safety instructions, and the rental counter's walkthrough, before any pass.

The rental sheet says the grinder takes four inches a pass. The stump says otherwise. Stump Math does the honest arithmetic: how many passes your depth really takes, how many bags of chips the stump becomes, and whether the afternoon holds the whole job.

Three panels. The depth truth divides the inches below grade by the claimed pass. The chip truth turns the stump's cylinder into bags. The afternoon truth multiplies stumps by minutes and tells you what kind of day it is.
